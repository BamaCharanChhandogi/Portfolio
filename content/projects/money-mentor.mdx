## The Problem

Most budgeting apps are just digital receipt books: you enter what you spent, and they show you a list. But they don't help you understand your spending patterns or give advice on how to actually save money before the month ends. If you're managing expenses with family or roommates, coordinating who paid what is even more chaotic.

## How Money Mentor Solves It

Money Mentor gives you a live financial dashboard that does two main things:
1. **Live Family Budgeting**: Whenever someone logs an expense, it updates instantly across everyone's screen using WebSockets.
2. **AI Spending Review**: Instead of just raw charts, Google Gemini reviews your last 30 days of expenses and gives you straightforward, human advice on where your money is going and what to cut back on.

## How It's Built

- **React & Tailwind CSS**: Fast, responsive frontend dashboard with dark and light themes.
- **Node.js, Express & MongoDB**: REST API backend with secure JWT authentication and password hashing.
- **Socket.io**: Real-time synchronization so shared expense rooms update without refreshing.
- **Google Gemini**: Formats raw monthly expense numbers into an easy-to-read financial health score.

## What It Means

A complete full-stack web application that solved real everyday budgeting headaches by combining live multi-user sync with practical AI insights.
