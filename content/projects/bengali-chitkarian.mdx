## Problem

Bengali students at Chitkara University needed a simple digital space around shared identity, events, and campus context. A generic social page would not feel specific enough for that community.

## What I Built

Bengali-Chitkarian is a React-based community website for Bengali students at Chitkara. The page presents the group, its story, and the reasons for joining in a focused student-facing experience.

## Technical Decisions

I kept the implementation front-end focused so the project could stay fast, easy to deploy, and simple for students to access. The design centers the community message rather than heavy application mechanics.

## Outcome

The project became a lightweight campus-community hub and a practical exercise in building a page for a specific audience rather than a generic portfolio demo.
