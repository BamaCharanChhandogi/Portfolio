## The Problem

When writing college essays, job applications, or tweets, you constantly have to check word or character limits. Opening heavy word processors just to check a character count is slow, and random online counters are filled with annoying ads.

## How It Solves It

This is a clean, ad-free text utility built in React. You paste or type your text, and it immediately calculates:
- Total words and characters (with and without spaces).
- Number of sentences and paragraphs.
- Estimated reading and speaking time.
- Quick buttons to convert text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, or copy to clipboard.

## How It's Built

- **Derived React State**: Calculates all numbers instantly on every keystroke without lag.
- **Regex Parsing**: Accurately counts words even with punctuation, hyphenated terms, and multiple spaces.
- **Clipboard API**: One-click button to copy modified text back to your clipboard.

## What It Means

A simple, practical tool that practiced controlled inputs and real-time text analysis in React.
